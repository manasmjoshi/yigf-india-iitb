Key Takeaways

What the data tells us

Observations and patterns from the registration data that inform programme design.

🌍

Geographic diversity spans multiple Indian states and international participants, reflecting YIGF India's national and regional reach within the Asia-Pacific internet governance ecosystem.

⚖️

The multistakeholder balance — academia, civil society, technical community, government, and private sector — demonstrates genuine cross-sector engagement rather than single-constituency dominance.

👥

Gender representation in the registrant pool provides a benchmark for targeted outreach in future editions to strengthen inclusivity.

📊

The age profile confirms YIGF India's positioning as a youth-led space, with the majority of registrants in the 18–30 bracket, while maintaining openness to a broader age range.
